Who I am

I'm Thibault. A few years ago, I was building what looked like a successful career in digital marketing, working with international brands while living under constant stress without even realizing it.

Everything changed when I discovered breathwork before COVID. I was simply looking for a way out of a really dark period. Instead, I found something that transformed how I lived. I became calmer, more focused, slept better, and showed up differently in my relationships. I wasn't just managing stress anymore. I felt present again.

The biggest change wasn't simply managing stress better. I stopped being physically present while mentally somewhere else. I listened differently, connected differently, and started feeling part of my own life again.

That experience sparked a passion. I spent years studying breathwork, heart-rate coherence, nervous system regulation, and human performance while experimenting on myself long before teaching others. Along the way, I completed two 10-day Vipassana silent retreats, along with other immersive experiences that deepened my understanding of awareness, presence, and the mind-body connection.

Between 2019 and 2021, while living in Kuala Lumpur, friends began asking for help. Soon, those conversations turned into paid coaching and became one of the most meaningful experiences of my life.

Today, I still work in digital marketing, but I also help people who feel constantly "on" reconnect with a calmer, more resilient version of themselves through practical, science-backed tools.

RESET is the method I wish I'd had when I was living in survival mode. It combines years of personal exploration, professional training, and coaching into a practical framework that helps people move from chronic stress to presence.
